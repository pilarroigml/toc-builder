# Ideas for later

Parked on 6 October 2026, to revisit after v1 works (after Step 7). None of these need AI, accounts or a backend.

## Why we need an edge

Alternatives are specialised ToC software (often paid, built for evaluators), general tools like Miro, Canva or donor Word templates (blank canvas, no guidance), and AI chat tools (fast, plausible, often weak on logic and evidence). v1's edge is free and private use, sourced guidance where you write, a quality checklist, and a donor-ready logframe. That's real but modest.

## Ideas

1. **"Read it back" story mode** (strong candidate). Turn the user's chain into plain sentences, e.g. "If we train 40 teachers, then 1,500 pupils are taught at their level, so that reading improves, assuming…". Gaps become obvious when read as a story. Simple to build.
2. **"What the evidence says" cards** (strong candidate, hardest to copy). Short cards at each level pointing to evaluated programmes (J-PAL, IPA, World Bank Africa Gender Innovation Lab, RAND) with what was found and links. Plays to content quality.
3. **"Spot the mistake" practice.** A quick quiz using the weak and strong examples already written. Playful, good for students and newcomers, low cost.
4. **Funder's eye view.** Show the ToC as a reviewer would, with the quality checklist as a score. Serves the "reviewer or funder" user from the brief.
