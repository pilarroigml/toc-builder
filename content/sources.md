## Approach to examples

Guidance examples are invented, but modelled on real programme designs documented by impact evaluation teams (J-PAL, IPA, World Bank Africa Gender Innovation Lab and similar). The evaluation page each one draws on is cited. No real organisation names or real figures are presented as the example's own.

## Reuse terms

Nothing has been reused as a list, taxonomy or framework structure so far, only paraphrased ideas with links. Reuse terms for NPC and BetterEvaluation still need checking before any structure from them is reused.
